## Style rules applied

JDMS currently specifies Sage Vancouver style. The v1.2 manuscript therefore applies:

1. references numbered in order of appearance;
2. square-bracket numeric citations retained in text;
3. reference-list entries end with full points;
4. more than three authors may be shortened to first three + `et al.`;
5. journal titles abbreviated where the standard abbreviation is unambiguous;
6. DOI presented as `DOI: ...`;
7. live websites include an access date;
8. policy reports/standards are formatted as reports/standards rather than journal articles.
