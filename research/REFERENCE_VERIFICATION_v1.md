## A. Authoritative policy / report references — verified

### [1] RAND enterprise Army M&S modernization

Hargrove H, Conley T, Allendorf E, Whitehead NP, Willcox J. *A Modernized Enterprise Army Modeling and Simulation Concept*. RAND Corporation. 2025. RRA3261-1.

Verified against RAND-hosted report. The report covers modernization across acquisition, analysis, experimentation, intelligence, T&E and training and describes MOSA, containerized models/simulations as services, enterprise event control and migration of preexisting models/simulations while maintaining continuity/pedigree.

Status: **VERIFIED**.

### [2] DoD MOSA

U.S. Department of Defense, OUSD(R&E), Systems Engineering and Architecture. *Modular Open Systems Approach*.

Verified official description: modular, loosely coupled, highly cohesive system structure; accepted standards and verifiable conformance; components can be added, modified, replaced or removed across the acquisition lifecycle.

Status: **VERIFIED**.

### [3] MOSA Implementation Guidebook

OUSD(R&E). *Implementing a Modular Open Systems Approach in DoD Programs*. 27 Feb 2025.

Verified official guidebook statement that MOSA supports use of consensus standards and severable components that can be incrementally added, removed or replaced throughout the lifecycle.

Status: **VERIFIED**.

### [4] MIL-STD-3022

U.S. Department of Defense. *MIL-STD-3022: Documentation of Verification, Validation, and Accreditation (VV&A) for Models and Simulations*.

Verified in ASSIST QuickSearch. Scope establishes templates for four core products: Accreditation Plan, V&V Plan, V&V Report and Accreditation Report, with common information intended to be shared across products.

Status: **VERIFIED**.

### [5] DoDM 5000.102

U.S. Department of Defense. *DoDM 5000.102: Modeling and Simulation Verification, Validation, and Accreditation for Operational Test and Evaluation and Live Fire Test and Evaluation*. Effective 9 Dec 2024.

Verified on DoD Directives site and official PDF.

Status: **VERIFIED**.

## D. Reference-use discipline

Use RAND/DoD sources for:

```text
enterprise modernization context
MOSA definition / replaceability
VV&A policy / documentation
T&E-specific intended-use requirements
```

Use academic sources for:

```text
model reuse/composability
continuous/lifecycle VV&A
validation use cases
credibility practice
interoperability standards gaps
```

Do not use cross-domain healthcare sources as evidence that TMSU works in defense T&E; use them only to show broader convergence on versioning/context/credibility/reuse principles.
